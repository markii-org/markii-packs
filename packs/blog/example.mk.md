::blog_header{title="Shipping Slowly" subtitle="On not rushing releases" author="Ada Lovelace" date="2026-08-30"}

Most of the delays I regret weren't caused by moving too slowly. They were
caused by moving fast toward the wrong thing and then having to walk it
back in public.

:::blog_pullquote{cite="Ada Lovelace"}
A release you can explain in one sentence is a release you actually
understand.
:::

That's not a rule against speed. It's a rule against skipping the part
where you check what you're about to ship.

:::blog_aside{label="Note"}
This post is about release pacing, not about testing strategy. Those are
related but worth keeping separate.
:::

Write the changelog entry before you write the code. If you can't, the
change is still too vague to ship.
